# CellIQ: friend feedback kit

Goal: find out whether future nurses and doctors (and NEET aspirants) would use an AI tool like this, what worries them, and what would make them trust it. This is opinion and usability feedback, NOT clinical validation. Say so in the write-up ("feedback from pre-med / nursing students").

## PART 1. Message to send your friends (copy and paste)

Hey! We built CellIQ for a hackathon. It is an AI helper that sorts lung scans so a doctor can look at the urgent ones first. It does NOT diagnose and it only uses fake demo patients. Could you spend 5 minutes trying it (or watching a demo) and answer a short form? I want your honest opinion, including "I wouldn't use this". Nothing you say will be quoted with your name unless you say it's OK.

## PART 2. What they should try (3 minutes)

1. Log in as a doctor (the demo account you give them).
2. Open a demo patient and upload a sample stained lung slide.
3. Look at the risk score, the priority tier and the heatmap.
4. Open the CT nodule page and click a flagged spot.
5. Read the drafted report and the "not for diagnosis" notice.

If they cannot reach the app, do it on a screen share and let them tell you where to click.

## PART 3. The form (paste into Google Forms, 1 to 5 scale unless noted)

About you (optional)
- Which best describes you? (Nursing student / Pre-med or medical student / NEET aspirant / Other)
- How comfortable are you with AI tools? (1 to 5)

First impression
1. In one sentence, what do you think this app does? (short answer)
2. How clear was the priority tier (Routine, Priority review, High priority)? (1 to 5)
3. How helpful was the heatmap? (1 to 5) Why? (short answer)

Would you use it?
4. If it were proven accurate, would you use a tool like this in your work or studies? (Definitely not / Probably not / Not sure / Probably / Definitely)
5. Would you trust the score? (1 to 5) What would make you trust it more? (short answer)
6. Would you rather the AI (pick one): only sort the scans / sort and highlight areas / suggest a diagnosis
7. Who should be responsible if the AI is wrong? (short answer)

Worries and gaps
8. What worries you most about AI in scans? (Wrong results / Doctors relying on it too much / Patient privacy / Bias / Cost / Other)
9. What is missing that a doctor would need before acting on it? (short answer)
10. Did the "research prototype, not for diagnosis" message come across clearly? (1 to 5)

Wrap up
11. One thing you liked. (short answer)
12. One thing you would change. (short answer)
13. May we quote your comments anonymously (for example "a nursing student said...")? (Yes / No)

## PART 4. Ground rules for you

- Use only the fake demo patients. Never let anyone upload real patient scans.
- Do not claim clinical validation or "reviewed by doctors".
- Keep every answer, including negative ones, and note how many people answered.
- Put "what we changed after feedback" in the write-up. Judges like it.
- Before giving friends their own access to a hosted version, change the demo passwords and set CELLIQ_JWT_SECRET.
